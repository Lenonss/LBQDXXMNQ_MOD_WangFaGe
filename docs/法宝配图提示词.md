# 三柄本命飞剑 · GPT 配图提示词

> 对象：灵析剑 / 灵源剑 / 灵道剑（本命法宝）。用途：更新公告、创意工坊、物品图鉴卡。
> 统一设定：**三柄同形，只换「剑首珠内的光」与「剑身纹路」**——这是它们看起来是一套、又能一眼区分的关键。

## 0. 为什么这么定

游戏里三柄剑共用剑形贴图（`object_PTjian01` / `object_MBjian01`），配方又都以衍天珠为核。所以视觉上：
**同一把三尺直剑，剑首嵌一颗衍天珠**；差异全在珠内异象与剑身蚀刻。

| 法宝 | 珠内 | 剑身 | 主光色 | 情绪 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 灵析剑 | 旋转星图 / 网格 | 满布细密刻度与符文，像一把量天之尺 | 冷银蓝 | 冷静、目录感 |
| 灵源剑 | 液态灵气的旋涡 | 剑脊一道中空灵脉，青白灵液流动 | 青碧 | 丰沛、流动 |
| 灵道剑 | 一缕盘着的墨色道行 | 由柄至尖 墨黑→白热渐变，49 道刻痕 | 墨 + 冷白 | 克制、锋利 |

```

Double-edged slim blade, narrow rectangular guard of pale carved jade, grip wrapped in dark silk cord, round pommel socket holding a single luminous pearl no larger than a pigeon egg. The pearl is the only saturated light source in the frame; everything else is ink, paper and shadow.

Visual style: Chinese gongbi fine-brush object painting fused with subtle digital rendering, painted on aged rice paper. Muted ink blacks and warm paper off-white, one dominant qi colour, fine paper grain, soft top light, gentle vignette. Restrained, museum-plate calm, no text, no border.
```

A few torn fragments of ancient scroll drift around the blade, each catching the light, orbiting slowly. Cold, precise, catalogued. Dominant colour: pale silver-blue.

## 5. 负面清单（追加末尾）

```
Avoid: text, letters, characters, watermark, logo, signature; western crossguard or katana curve; glowing eyes; overexposed bloom; extra blades; hands with wrong finger count; clutter; neon colours; 3D render plastic look.
```

## 6. 出图与用法

- **尺寸**：图鉴/工坊缩略图用 `1024x1024`；公告头图再加一版 `1536x1024`（同提示词 + `wider composition, sword at right third, empty paper on the left`）。
- **抠图**：宣纸底可直接用，不必去背；若要透明底，让模型出 `plain flat dark grey background, no shadow`，再在 PS 里用「选择主体」。
- **游戏内 128×128 图标**：GPT 出图缩到 128 会糊掉剑纹，建议只用于宣传；图标要么手工重绘，要么同提示词加 `flat game icon, thick silhouette, three-quarter view, plain dark background` 单独出一版再压暗。
- **三张并排验收**：轮廓是否完全一致？珠内异象是否各自成立？是否只有一种主光色？缩到 200px 还分得清哪把是灵道剑？

## 九、v3：扔掉「纸底隐喻」（两次实生都像杂志插页的根因）

问题不在模型，在底稿结构。`Object plate` + `aged rice paper ground` + 四周留白，等于要求模型**画一张装裱好的图版**；它必然补上纸边、投影与印痕，于是无论剑画得多准，整体都读作「杂志内页」。器物图的主体是物，纸张只能是背景，不能让纸反客为主。

v3 改用摄影语汇，直接复制：

```
Museum artifact photograph. One Chinese straight sword (jian) stands upright, point up, its tip resting on a polished black stone plinth. Matte neutral grey seamless background, seamless studio sweep; no paper, no border, no mount, no page shadow.

The sword: 100 cm overall, blade 78 cm, straight and double-edged, 3.2 cm at the guard tapering to a point, with a pronounced central ridge. Dark polished steel, faint hammer marks, edge slightly worn; it reads as dense metal, opaque, not translucent. [母题差异行——见下]

Hilt, in order from the blade: rectangular guard of pale carved jade, 6 cm wide; grip of dark silk cord in visible crossed wrapping, 18 cm; bronze pommel with a round socket holding one pigeon-egg pearl set flush, only its front hemisphere showing, lit softly from within and dimmer than the blade.

Lighting: one large softbox at 45 degrees upper left, plus a hard rim light along the right edge of the blade; deep falloff, faint reflection on the plinth.
Camera: 85 mm, f/8, whole sword in frame, 20 percent empty space above the tip.
No text, no watermark, no illustration, no painting style, no paper texture, no origami, no shards.
```

三条硬约束别删：`matte neutral grey seamless`（把纸换成无缝背景）、`opaque, not translucent`（压玻璃感）、`dimmer than the blade`（把主次掰回剑上）。

母题差异行（换这句即可出另两把）：
- 灵析剑：`Engraved on the blade from guard to tip: hair-fine measuring scales, tiny numerals, constellation dots, and a single straight line of small glowing nodes along the ridge; one accent colour, pale silver-blue.`
- 灵源剑：`A hollow channel runs down the blade spine, filled with flowing green-white fluid, condensation beads on the steel; inside the pearl, a slow whirlpool of liquid jade light; one accent colour, jade green.`
- 灵道剑：`The blade shades from ink black at the guard to white heat at the tip; forty-nine fine notches are cut along one edge; inside the pearl, a coiled thread of ink-black qi; one accent colour, cold white.`

**图生图底图怎么选**：挑「正面竖立、背景干净、剑身占画面 70%」的真实古剑高清照，比任何像素图标都有效。检索词：`越王勾践剑 高清`、`汉代 玉具剑 博物馆`、`Chinese jian museum artifact`。

## 十、为什么拿不到游戏官方贴图

- `object_PTjian01` / `object_MBjian01` 打包在 Unity 资源包（`AmazingCultivationSimulator_Data`）内，安装目录里**没有散落的 png**：对整棵安装树搜 `*jian*`，只命中 `Scripts/Skill/class/zhen/zhen_jointjian.lua`；游戏根目录也没有 `Resources/` 文件夹（模组的 `Resources/` 是模组自己的）。
- 解包需要 AssetStudio / UnityPy，而本机 shell 执行被策略拒绝，跑不了外部脚本。
- 即便解出来，原版法宝图标是 128 px 级像素图，作 1024 级底图只会把分辨率与材质带偏。它的正确用途是**吸色**：从图标取 3–5 个主色写进 palette 行，而不是当构图参考。

要真拿游戏内实拍，只有一条路：进游戏打开法宝详情，`Win+Shift+S` 截大图——但游戏内预览本身也受贴图分辨率限制。

## 十一、以原版像素剑贴图为底：黑场灵光向（2026-11-20 实拍后新增）

用户提供的游戏内原始精灵图（1338×1175，纯黑底，45° 斜置，像素画）：剑身浅灰紫、扁平长条、带中脊浅槽；格为扁平椭圆横档（藕粉）；柄为绯红绳缠、粗壮方块状；首为银灰圆帽；剑锋带一圈很细的蓝色描边。全图无护手纹饰、无珠、无刻纹。

**这张图在 img2img 时会带进三样东西，必须显式抵消**：
1. 45° 斜置 → 出图仍是斜的，除非指定 vertical 或先把底图垫进竖版黑画布；
2. 像素块、平涂明暗、蓝色描边 → 会变成「8-bit 风」；
3. 纯黑底 → 模型可能加地面、加投影、加光晕，把黑底弄脏。

**同时它有一个免费的礼物**：纯黑底 = 天然暗场。与其换纸底、换灰底，不如顺势做「暗场灵光」——剑悬在纯黑里，母题只用光色区分。这比摄影底更容易出系列感，也天然掩盖像素底图的分辨率缺陷。

### 关键预处理（不做这步必出横图）

把精灵图居中贴进 1024×1536 的纯黑画布（四周补黑，剑只占中间约 60%），再喂给模型。底图比例与目标比例一致，模型才不会按 1338×1175 的横向构图生成。

### 主提示词

```
Reference image: a low-resolution pixel-art sword sprite on pure black, diagonal. Keep only its silhouette and proportions: blade-to-grip ratio, the flat oval guard, the thick wrapped grip, the rounded pommel. Do not keep the pixel grid, the flat shading, the blue edge outline, or the diagonal tilt.

Recompose as a vertical, point-up painting on a pure black background (RGB 0,0,0). Whole blade in frame, fifteen percent empty space above the tip, no floor, no plinth, no shadow, no vignette.

Blade: 78 cm, straight, double-edged, 3.2 cm at the guard tapering to a point, pronounced central ridge. Hand-painted dark steel, fine hammer marks, worn edge; one thin cool rim light runs the whole length. Opaque and dense; not glass, not translucent, no transparency.

Hilt, in order: flat oval guard of pale carved jade; grip of dark crimson silk cord in visible crossed wrapping; bronze pommel with a round socket holding one pigeon-egg pearl set flush, only its front hemisphere showing, glowing softly from within, dimmer than the blade.

[MOTIF]

Rendering: clean hand-painted 2D game asset for a dark inventory screen, sharp silhouette, painterly brush texture at high resolution, single soft key light from upper left plus the pearl's own glow.
No pixelation, no pixel grid, no black outline, no cel shading, no text, no watermark, no background objects.
```

### 三把的母题行

- 灵析剑：`Along the blade from guard to tip, hair-fine measuring scales and constellation dots etched and faintly lit in pale silver-blue; one straight line of small glowing nodes down the ridge; a few thin constellation threads drift in the black around the blade.`
- 灵源剑：`A hollow channel down the blade spine filled with flowing green-white fluid, condensation beads on the steel; inside the pearl a slow whirlpool of liquid jade light; thin light threads curl off the blade and dissolve.`
- 灵道剑：`Blade shaded from ink black at the guard to white heat at the tip; forty-nine fine notches cut along one edge; inside the pearl a coiled thread of ink-black qi; a faint ink haze drifts in the black around the blade.`

### 参数

- 底图强度（denoise / image weight）：0.55–0.70 保轮廓；超过 0.75 轮廓会漂，低于 0.45 会留像素块。
- 尺寸 1024×1536；先出灵析剑，定稿后**以它为参考图**续生另两把，不要每把都从像素图起手——否则三张的光位与剑形比例对不上。
- 需要透明底做图标：把最后一句改成 `pure black background, hard object edge, for keying`，抠黑比抠灰稳。
- 若工具支持 negative prompt：`pixel art, 8-bit, low resolution, jpeg artifacts, glow outline, drop shadow, floor, gradient background, text`。

### 变体：要灰底摄影版

把两句换掉即可：`pure black background (RGB 0,0,0)` → `matte neutral grey seamless studio sweep`；并补 `lighting: one large softbox at 45 degrees upper left, hard rim light on the right edge, faint reflection on a polished black plinth`。其余不动。

## 十二、风格锁：这不是「写实」，是 PBR 渲染（2026-11-20 第 4 轮返修）

第 4 轮成图（1024×1536，黑底竖版）的问题逐条：
1. 剑身是 PBR 金属——有环境倒影带、渐变镜面高光、拉丝横纹，一眼 3D 渲染；
2. 刻度被画成机械加工的细横线，读作「拉丝不锈钢」，不像符文；
3. 玉格膨胀成厚圆盘（象牙白、3D 浮雕云纹），宽度接近剑身 3 倍，抢视觉；
4. 缠柄是亮红绸缎的次表面散射质感，太新太亮；
5. 首珠是摄影级球体 + 写实金托，亮度又是全场最高；
6. 画幅太满，剑几乎顶到上下边，要求的「上方留 15% 空」没执行；
7. 剑身偏宽短（宽高约 1:9），三尺剑应为 1:22–1:25。

**根因**：提示词里 `dark steel / fine hammer marks / worn edge / high resolution` 这组词把模型推向「材质渲染」，而参考图是像素图、提供不了笔触，模型只能用默认的写实渲染来补。要压住它，必须显式写**风格锁**，并且把材质描述改成平涂语言。

### 风格锁（每张图都粘这一段）

```
STYLE LOCK — soft flat-shaded 2D game item art, in the manner of an illustrated cultivation-game inventory icon: matte gouache with light airbrush gradients, three to five flat value bands per material, occlusion drawn as a soft painted shadow rather than a texture, a thin pale outline along the sunlit edge, muted desaturated palette plus one saturated accent, one soft glow around the pearl and the etched light.
No metallic reflections, no environment map, no chrome, no specular highlights, no subsurface scattering, no depth of field, no bokeh, no 3D render, no photoreal material, no camera vignette.
```

### 材质改成平涂语言

- 剑身：`pale blue-grey steel painted as three flat bands — shadow, mid, highlight — with a soft gradient; the light down the ridge is a thin tapered glow line, not a reflection; 3.2 cm at the guard, 78 cm long, ratio about 1:24.`
- 玉格：`pastel jade, softly lit from upper left, cloud scroll incised as a single fine line; width no more than 1.6 times the blade width.`
- 缠柄：`crimson silk, matte, flat crossed wraps with one soft shadow band between each, no sheen.`
- 首珠：`soft glowing sphere, one flat highlight disc, gold setting reduced to two flat tones.`
- 构图：`the sword occupies 62 percent of the frame height, twenty percent empty black above the tip.`

### 救现图：只改画风（保留构图与设计）

以第 4 轮那张为参考图，强度 0.35–0.45：

```
Keep the exact composition, silhouette, proportions and palette of the reference image, and keep the design: the star-etched blade, the jade guard, the red wrapped grip, the pearl-set pommel, the constellation threads.
Redraw everything in the STYLE LOCK above. Convert the steel from photoreal metal into flat painted bands; remove all environment reflections and specular streaks; flatten the guard carving; matte the silk; simplify the gold to two flat tones; keep the glow.
Same framing, same pure black background, same vertical orientation.
```

负面词：`photo, photoreal, 3D render, PBR, chrome, metal reflection, specular highlight, bokeh, depth of field, HDR, realistic jewelry, octane render, unreal engine, wide blade, short sword, gladius`。

### 最有效的一招：拿 mod 自己的图当风格参考

`Resources/Spr/Object/wfg_item_yantianzhu.png`（128×128，蓝光球）与 `wfg_item_yantianpei.png`（128×128，绿玉牌红穗）就是本项目已定的画风：平涂、柔光、饱和主色、无金属反射、无景深。若工具支持多参考图 / IP-Adapter，把 `wfg_item_yantianzhu.png` 作为**风格参考**同时喂入，比任何形容词都准。

### 注意：进游戏的 128 图标要另出

上面这套出的是宣传/图鉴级大图。真正进 `Resources/Spr/Object/` 当物品图标的是 128×128，细节会糊成一片——需要单独出简化版：只留外轮廓、格、柄、珠四点识别特征，去掉星图与刻痕，全图 3 色以内。
